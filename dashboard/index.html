Every protocol still waiting on its token, ranked by what the launch would be worth.

The board starts from the forty protocols with at least $50M of TVL that record no token, then adds every smaller launch candidate the snapshot holds. Each one is valued from its own figures — its TVL, its seven-day growth, and how long it has run with no token — against the launched protocols in its own category. Implied value is this entry’s own claim, never a price, and never a prediction of whether or when a token is issued.

Honest language: a protocol “records no token” when the snapshot records none for it. That is not proof it has none.

How the value is worked out

Implied value = TVL × comparable multiple × growth multiplier × tenure multiplier. The comparable multiple is the median market-cap / TVL of the launched protocols in the same category — at least three launched peers, or the market-wide median when a category has fewer. Growth is the seven-day TVL change, clamped to −50% … +100% and weighted 0.6. Tenure runs from 0.85 for a protocol tracked a day to 1.15 for one tracked four years with no token. The same function scores every row; no protocol name is ever special-cased.
